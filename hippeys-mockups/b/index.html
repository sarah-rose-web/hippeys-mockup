Fifty-seven years on Railroad Street.

Ring bologna, franks and meat sticks, smoked over real hickory by the same family since 1969.

The family record

1969

Jay Hippey and George Sirianni buy the old Leid's Meats building on Railroad Street.

Then

Ring bologna and franks sold under the Hippey's name, smoked over hickory, tied by hand.

Today

George's grandson, Matt Forney, runs the plant. Same building. Same hickory.

HOW WE MAKE IT

Some smoked meat is smoked with a liquid. Ours is smoked with wood: real hickory, the way it's been done in this building since 1969. Beef comes from Dillsburg, pork from Souderton, and every casing is still tied by hand.
